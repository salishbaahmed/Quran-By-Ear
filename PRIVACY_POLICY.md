# Privacy Policy for Quran-By-Ear

**Last Updated:** August 5, 2026

Welcome to **Quran-By-Ear**. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy notice or our practices with regard to your personal information, please contact the developer.

## 1. What Information Do We Collect?

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the App, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and services, or otherwise contact us.

The personal information that we collect depends on the context of your interactions with us and the App, the choices you make, and the products and features you use. The personal information we collect may include the following:

*   **Account Information:** When you create an account, we collect your email address and password. This is required to save your preferences and track your progress across devices.
*   **Usage Data & Telemetry:** We collect anonymous or pseudonymous data regarding your usage of the App. This includes statistics such as which Surahs or Ayahs you have listened to and the total time listened. This helps us improve the app experience.
*   **Device Permissions (Storage):** The App requests access to your device's local storage solely for the purpose of downloading and saving Quran recitation audio files (`.mp3`) for offline listening. We do not access, read, or modify any other personal files on your device.

## 2. How Do We Use Your Information?

We use personal information collected via our App for a variety of business purposes described below. We process your personal information for these purposes in reliance on our legitimate business interests, in order to enter into or perform a contract with you, with your consent, and/or for compliance with our legal obligations:

*   **To facilitate account creation and logon process.**
*   **To manage user accounts.**
*   **To deliver and facilitate delivery of services to the user.** (e.g., saving your listening progress).
*   **To improve the App.** We use telemetry data to understand how the app is being used and identify areas for improvement.

## 3. Will Your Information Be Shared With Anyone?

We only share information with the following third-party service providers that perform services for us or on our behalf and require access to such information to do that work:

*   **Supabase:** We use Supabase for our backend infrastructure, user authentication, and database hosting. Your account information and telemetry data are stored securely on their servers. You can read their privacy policy at [https://supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy).
*   **Quran.com API:** We use the public Quran.com API to fetch Quranic text and audio files. Your IP address may be exposed to their servers when your device downloads audio directly from their Content Delivery Network (CDN).

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

## 4. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We will only keep your personal information for as long as it is necessary for the purposes set out in this privacy notice, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law (such as tax, accounting, or other legal requirements). 

When we have no ongoing legitimate business need to process your personal information, we will either delete or anonymize such information.

## 5. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. Specifically, user passwords are encrypted and hashed by our authentication provider (Supabase), and data in transit is protected via HTTPS. However, despite our safeguards and efforts to secure your information, no electronic transmission over the Internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

## 6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to the personal information we collect from you, change that information, or delete it in some circumstances. To request to review, update, or delete your personal information, please contact the developer or use the account deletion options within the App (if available).

## 7. Updates to This Notice

We may update this privacy notice from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Last Updated" date and the updated version will be effective as soon as it is accessible. We encourage you to review this privacy notice frequently to be informed of how we are protecting your information.
